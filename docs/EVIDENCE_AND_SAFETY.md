# Evidence classes, lifecycle and hardware boundaries

Hardware means an actual measured device result with source/image/configuration and cleanup attribution. Static means source/binary reasoning on a specific version/hash. Recorded/Offline means computation on saved real inputs, or explicitly labelled simulation. Inferred means a hypothesis not established by the other classes. Every new claim needs its source and limits.

No hardware operation is authorized by reading this handoff. In the present bench scope, fresh direct human readiness must establish tracked HMD, both controllers OFF, first tracker powered/stable and other trackers OFF only after the exact candidate/recovery is prepared. Do not generate confirmation files or recycle consumed readiness. Future joint-controller validation must preserve its measured150ms controller guard.

Use one exclusive radio/SRAM owner. Before another profile, the actual matching invocation must report literal true for session_closed_verified, ram_restored_verified, mpu_restored and trace_controls_restored_verified. A host-side success flag is not a substitute. Never kill an owner while it still holds SRAM. Stock Valve flash remains untouched; reset/powercycle invalidates temporary installation.

Bind target, CRC/source slot/tag, nonce, mode revision/N/pending state, finite lease, exact image, exact invocation and current boot. A transport ACK is not applied firmware mode, LED pulse, physical darkness, geometry identity or pose validity. Successful transfer is not activation/recovery. Historical approved sessions are not permanent permission.

Keep age250ms, identity/48-of61, pixel/clock/covariance, IMU continuity and controller guards. Never replace missing data with frozen models, synthetic samples or old recorded presence and call it tracking. Simulated math tests are permissible only when explicitly labelled and never reported as real sensor delivery.

On a demonstrated failure: preserve the exact input/hash/observable/restoration, reproduce the actual defect offline where possible, change one supported cause, and prepare one bounded discriminator. Do not repeat an unchanged consumed probe, sweep phase blindly, weaken guards to produce green status or automatically restart/OTA.
